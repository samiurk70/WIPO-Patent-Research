WIPO patent-family series -- manual export from the IP Statistics Data Center
https://www3.wipo.int/ipstats/   retrieved 2026-09-02 by S.R. Khan

Indicator      : 6a - Patent family by origin
Report type    : Total count by applicant's origin
Attribution    : APPLICANT origin (matches the publication file already in the pipeline)
Year basis     : earliest filing (first-filing based), NOT publication year
Years exported : 2000-2024
Origins        : the 13 study economies WIPO lists. Timor-Leste returns no result and is
                 not selectable with data; Lao PDR is present but single-digit and sparse.
                 China, Hong Kong SAR was excluded -- separate origin, not in the set.
Source stamp   : "WIPO statistics database. Last updated: May 2026"

Read by pipeline/load_wipo_families.py, which records two assumptions:
  - a blank cell is zero families that year, EXCEPT where a year is blank for every
    origin (2024), which is not-yet-reported and is dropped;
  - RQ1 uses 2000-2022 (WINDOW_END). 2023 carries artefactual country values
    (Philippines 7% of its own preceding five-year mean, Indonesia 862%).

NOTE the year basis. This series is anchored on earliest filing; the publication file is
anchored on publication year. They are different measurements, not two views of one
count, and Section 3.2 must say so.
